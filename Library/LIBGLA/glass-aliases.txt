! Zoa glass aliases: other names for glass catalogs and glasses.
!
! Read from LIBGLA/ in every search-path folder and from the program's
! glass folder; the first definition of an alias wins, so a file of your
! own in a search-path LIBGLA/ folder can add aliases or override these.
!
!   CAT <alias> <catalog>            a catalog name that means another
!                                    catalog, anywhere a catalog is named
!   GLA <alias> <catalog> <glass>    a glass name given without a catalog
!                                    (GLA S2 SK16) that means this glass
!
! Lenses saved before the Schott catalogs were merged name SCH2000 and
! SCH2023; both are now the one SCHOTT catalog.
CAT SCH2000 SCHOTT
CAT SCH2023 SCHOTT
!
! The KDP-2 MATL catalog of special materials is now SPECIAL, and its
! other material names mean these SPECIAL materials.
CAT MATL    SPECIAL

GLA IRTRAN6  SPECIAL CDTE
GLA PLYSTY   SPECIAL POLYSTYRENE
GLA POLYCARB SPECIAL POLYCARBONATE
